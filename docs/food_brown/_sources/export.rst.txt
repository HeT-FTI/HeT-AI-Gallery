_`Experimental Notes`
=====================

This page summarizes observed S-value dynamics for oven-baked chicken wings and provides practical hints for threshold design.

_`Stage-by-Stage Notes`
-----------------------

1. Initial dip: protein denaturation and surface moisture film reduce color purity.
2. Fast rise: stronger Maillard reaction and oil film increase perceived saturation.
3. Plateau: color stabilizes in a golden-brown region and often maps to the best serving window.
4. Decline: charring area increases and the S-value falls again.

_`Engineering Recommendations`
------------------------------

- Use moving-window smoothing on global S-mean before detecting turning points.
- Treat the plateau-end decline turning point as a candidate overbake trigger.
- Combine S-value trend with local char-area metrics to reduce false positives.
