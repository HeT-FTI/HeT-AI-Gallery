_`FAQ`
======

_`Why are images missing after build?`
--------------------------------------

This project distributes images through `docs/build.py`. Store final assets in `docs/images` instead of only in `docs/sphinx/images`.

_`Why is the logo missing?`
---------------------------

`conf.py` references both logo and favicon as fixed assets. Keep these files:

- `docs/images/OUT_logo.svg`
- `docs/images/ALL_logo.jpg`

_`How can I detect overbake quickly?`
-------------------------------------

Monitor the decline turning point at the end of the S-value plateau, and combine it with local char-area change using a dual-threshold rule.
